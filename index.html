Shrimp Scampi with Linguine

25 min · one skillet + pasta pot · Natasha's Kitchen · 4.98★ (118)

$6.11per plate · ×4 = $24.44
~534 kcalper plate · P ~35 g
25 minincl. pasta

Ingredients

  • 1.1 lb extra-large peeled, deveined shrimp
  • 12 oz 365 linguine (¾ of the box)
  • 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ cup chicken broth (in place of dry white wine; source option)
  • 1½ Tbsp lemon juice + wedges (1 lemon)
  • ¼ cup chopped Italian parsley
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (on hand)
  • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper (on hand)
  • ½ tsp each salt and black pepper (on hand), plus salt for the pasta water

Sauce is cooked as written (the source uses it for 1.5 lb shrimp), so there is enough to coat the pasta. No fish sauce.

Steps

  1. Start the pasta.Bring a big pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook 12 oz linguine (¾ of the box) per the package. Drain and keep warm while the shrimp cooks.
    12 oz linguineSalt for the water
  2. Prep the shrimp.Pat 1.1 lb peeled, deveined shrimp dry and season with ½ tsp salt and ½ tsp black pepper. Mince 4 garlic cloves, chop ¼ cup parsley, squeeze 1½ Tbsp lemon juice and cut the rest of the lemon into wedges.
    1.1 lb shrimp½ tsp salt½ tsp black pepper4 garlic cloves¼ cup parsley1 lemon
  3. Sear the shrimp.Heat 1 Tbsp olive oil and 1 Tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the shrimp in a single layer; cook 1–2 min per side until just opaque. Move to a plate right away so they don't overcook.
    1 Tbsp olive oil1 Tbsp butterSeasoned shrimp
  4. Build the sauce.Lower to medium. Add the other 1 Tbsp olive oil and 1 Tbsp butter. Sauté the garlic and ¼ tsp crushed red pepper 30 sec. Add ½ cup chicken broth (in place of white wine, as the source allows); simmer 2 min until reduced by half. Stir in the 1½ Tbsp lemon juice.
    1 Tbsp olive oil1 Tbsp butterGarlic¼ tsp crushed red pepper½ cup chicken broth1½ Tbsp lemon juice
  5. Finish the shrimp.Return the shrimp and their juices to the skillet and toss in the sauce. Take off the heat and stir in the parsley. Taste before adding salt, since the broth is already salted.
    ShrimpParsleySalt & pepper to taste
  6. Toss and serve.Toss the linguine with the shrimp and sauce. Sun Nov 22: serve two plates with lemon wedges. Chill the other two within 2 hr. Tue Nov 24: reheat gently in a skillet with a splash of broth until hot (165°F).
    Cooked linguineLemon wedges

Quick Chicken Tacos with Sauce

25 min · one skillet · RecipeTin Eats · 4.98★ (143)

$5.94per plate · ×4 = $23.76
~555 kcalper plate · P ~38 g
25 minno marinating

Ingredients

  • ~1.5 lb (700 g) boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 12 corn tortillas (365 stone-ground; 6 per night)
  • 1 cup chicken broth (2 Tbsp for the paste, the rest for the sauce)
  • 3 Tbsp lime juice (2 limes) + 1 lime in wedges
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced (1 per night)
  • 1 large avocado, sliced (½ per night)
  • Handful cilantro leaves
  • ¼ cup sour cream (1 Tbsp per plate)
  • 2½ Tbsp olive oil (on hand)
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste (on hand)
  • 2 tsp sugar (on hand)
  • Spices (on hand): 2 tsp each garlic powder, onion powder, dried oregano; 3 tsp each cumin, paprika; ½ tsp cayenne; 1 tsp salt; ½ tsp black pepper

Toppings are the source's tomato, avocado, cilantro and sour cream. Lettuce, cheese and onion are left off to keep the cart small and each plate under 600 kcal. No fish sauce.

Steps

  1. Make the taco paste.In a large bowl mix 2 tsp each garlic powder, onion powder and dried oregano, 3 tsp each cumin and paprika, ½ tsp cayenne, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper, 2 minced garlic cloves, 3 Tbsp lime juice (2 limes) and 1 Tbsp olive oil. Stir in 2 Tbsp of the 1 cup chicken broth to make a loose paste.
    2 tsp garlic powder2 tsp onion powder2 tsp oregano3 tsp cumin3 tsp paprika½ tsp cayenne1 tsp salt½ tsp black pepper2 garlic cloves3 Tbsp lime juice1 Tbsp olive oil2 Tbsp chicken broth
  2. Coat the chicken.Add ~1.5 lb chicken thighs (about 700 g, the whole pack) and turn to coat.
    ~1.5 lb chicken thighs
  3. Sear the chicken.Heat 1½ Tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the chicken 3 min per side until lightly seared. Keep the marinade left in the bowl. Move the chicken to a shallow dish.
    1½ Tbsp olive oilCoated chicken
  4. Make the sauce.Turn to medium. Add the rest of the broth (about ⅞ cup), 2 Tbsp tomato paste, 2 tsp sugar and the leftover marinade. Stir and scrape the pan, then bring to a simmer.
    ~⅞ cup chicken broth2 Tbsp tomato paste2 tsp sugarLeftover marinade
  5. Shred and simmer.Shred the chicken with two forks or chop it (fine if still raw inside). Return it to the sauce and simmer 2–3 min until the sauce is a thin syrup and the chicken reaches 165°F. Add a splash of water if it reduces too fast. Adjust salt.
    Seared chickenWater if needed
  6. Prep the toppings.For tonight: dice 1 Roma tomato, slice ½ avocado, chop a handful of cilantro and cut the third lime into wedges.
    1 Roma tomato½ avocadoCilantro1 lime
  7. Warm, fill and store.Warm 6 tortillas per the packet. Fill with chicken and plenty of sauce, then top with tomato, avocado, cilantro and 1 Tbsp sour cream per plate. Mon Nov 23: serve two plates (3 tacos each). Chill the rest of the filling within 2 hr. Wed Nov 25: reheat the filling to 165°F and build with the other 6 tortillas, tomato and avocado half.
    6 tortillas per nightChicken fillingToppings2 Tbsp sour cream per night
