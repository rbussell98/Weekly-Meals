Chilli Con Carne

40 min · one skillet · RecipeTin Eats · 4.9★ (338)

$4.08per plate · ×4 = $16.32
~562 kcalper plate, with rice
40 minquick cook

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 365 90% lean ground beef
  • 1 organic yellow onion, diced
  • 1 organic red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) fire-roasted crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (15.5 oz) dark red kidney beans, drained
  • 2 tsp Better Than Bouillon beef base (replaces 2 beef cubes)
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste (on hand)
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil (on hand)
  • 1½ tsp sugar (on hand)
  • ½ cup water
  • Spices (on hand): 4 tsp paprika, 5 tsp cumin, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 2 tsp oregano, 1–2 tsp cayenne, 1½ tsp salt
  • 2/3 cup dry jasmine rice (on hand) → ~2 cups cooked

Steps

  1. Make the spice mix.Mix 4 tsp paprika, 5 tsp cumin, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 2 tsp oregano, 1 tsp cayenne (up to 2 tsp for more heat) and 1½ tsp salt. Start 2/3 cup dry jasmine rice now so it is ready with the chili.
    4 tsp paprika5 tsp cumin2 tsp garlic powder2 tsp onion powder2 tsp oregano1–2 tsp cayenne1½ tsp salt2/3 cup dry jasmine rice
  2. Soften the aromatics.Heat 1 Tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add 3 minced garlic cloves and 1 diced onion; cook 1 min. Add 1 diced red bell pepper; cook 2 min until the onion is translucent.
    1 Tbsp olive oil3 garlic cloves1 onion1 red bell pepper
  3. Brown the beef.Turn heat to high. Add 1 lb ground beef; cook, breaking it up, until mostly browned.
    1 lb 90% lean ground beef
  4. Bloom the spices.Add the spice mix. Cook until the beef is browned all over; this releases the spice flavor.
    Spice mix
  5. Add the rest.Stir in 3 Tbsp tomato paste, the 28 oz can crushed tomatoes, 1 can kidney beans (drained), 2 tsp Better Than Bouillon beef base (in place of 2 beef cubes), 1½ tsp sugar and ½ cup water. Bring to a simmer.
    3 Tbsp tomato paste28 oz crushed tomatoes15.5 oz kidney beans, drained2 tsp beef base1½ tsp sugar½ cup water
  6. Simmer.Quick cook 20–40 min, uncovered, on medium-low so it bubbles gently. Taste and adjust salt just before serving; the beef base is salty, so taste first.
    Salt to taste
  7. Serve and store.Sun Nov 15: serve two bowls over ½ cup cooked rice each, with toppings you have. Cool the other two portions and chill within 2 hr. Tue Nov 17: reheat to 165°F.
    2 cups cooked rice (½ cup per plate)

Chicken Chow Mein

30 min · wok or big skillet · RecipeTin Eats · 4.97★ (359)

$5.56per plate · ×4 = $22.24
~554 kcalper plate
30 minincl. 10 min marinate

Ingredients

  • 14 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
  • ~400 g Hakubaku organic ramen (about 1½ of 2 packs)
  • 8 cups green cabbage, finely shredded (about 1 small head)
  • 2 carrots, julienned
  • 6 green onions, cut in 2-inch pieces
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 3 Tbsp oyster sauce
  • 3 Tbsp mirin (sub for Chinese cooking wine)
  • 3 Tbsp soy sauce (on hand)
  • 4 tsp cornstarch (on hand)
  • 2 tsp sugar (on hand)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (on hand)
  • 3 Tbsp peanut or other oil (on hand)
  • ½ cup water
  • Pinch black pepper (sub for white pepper)

Steps

  1. Mix the sauce.Mix 4 tsp cornstarch with 3 Tbsp soy sauce, then add 3 Tbsp oyster sauce, 3 Tbsp mirin, 2 tsp sugar (reduced for mirin), 1 tsp sesame oil and a pinch of black pepper.
    4 tsp cornstarch3 Tbsp soy sauce3 Tbsp oyster sauce3 Tbsp mirin2 tsp sugar1 tsp sesame oilblack pepper
  2. Marinate the chicken.Thinly slice 14 oz chicken thighs. Toss with 2 Tbsp of the sauce; set aside 10 min.
    14 oz chicken thighs2 Tbsp sauce
  3. Prep vegetables and noodles.Finely shred 8 cups cabbage. Julienne 2 carrots. Cut 6 green onions into 2-inch pieces; keep whites and greens apart. Finely chop 4 garlic cloves. Cook about 400 g ramen (1½ packs) per the packet and drain.
    8 cups cabbage2 carrots6 green onions4 garlic cloves~400 g ramen noodles
  4. Sear garlic and chicken.Work in two batches (half of everything each) so the wok is not crowded. Heat 1½ Tbsp peanut or other oil over high. Stir-fry half the garlic 10 sec. Add half the chicken; stir-fry about 1 min until browned outside.
    1½ Tbsp oil per batchGarlicMarinated chicken
  5. Wilt the vegetables.Add half the cabbage, carrot and green-onion whites. Stir-fry 1½ min until the cabbage is mostly wilted.
    CabbageCarrotGreen-onion whites
  6. Toss with noodles and sauce.Add half the noodles, half the remaining sauce and ¼ cup water. Toss constantly 1 min. Add half the green-onion greens; toss 30 sec. Make sure the chicken reaches 165°F. Repeat for batch two.
    NoodlesSauce¼ cup water per batchGreen-onion greens
  7. Serve and store.Mon Nov 16: serve two plates right away. Chill the other two within 2 hr. Wed Nov 18: reheat to 165°F with a splash of water.
    No additional ingredients
